01 · Overview

Turning a real operational need into a digital solution

This project focused on providing a structured way to reserve discussion rooms and help library staff oversee booking activity.

The challenge

Checking availability across rooms and time slots, preventing overlapping reservations, and keeping booking information organized.

The solution

A Microsoft Power Apps Canvas App integrated with SharePoint / Microsoft Lists to centralize booking and administrative workflows.
